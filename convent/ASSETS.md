## Sounds you can add

These recordings are **not included** (they were not available under a licence that allows
redistribution). The game works without them: it plays sounds made in code, or stays quiet for that
effect. To add your own, put files with these names in `public/assets/sounds/` (mp3, ogg or wav). Please
use sounds you are allowed to redistribute, for example CC0 ones.

`ambience`, `hum` (the ghosts' humming, `nun-hum`), `heartbeat`, `bell`, `ghost-whisper`, `ghost-boo`,
`jump-scare-2`, `door-slam`, `thunder`, `thunder-2`, `evil-laugh` (plays as the night starts, on the big
screen and every laptop). The big screen's pumpkin-patch music also looks for `nun-hum` and `heartbeat`.

## How the models are used

`public/assets/models/models.json` lists a model for each kind of furniture, the player costumes, the
zombies, and a `recolor` table that repaints the Kenney furniture colours in older, darker ones. The Count
and the Doctor's Monster are built from shapes in `public/js/actors.js`: the cartoon pack has nothing
frightening enough for them.
